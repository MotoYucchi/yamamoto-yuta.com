## Research Presentations
- **January 2026**: Presented at the Institute of Electronics, Information and Communication Engineers (IEICE), Circuits and Systems (CAS) Society.  
  [Paper Link (IEICE)](https://ken.ieice.org/ken/paper/20260123acqt/)

  **【Abstract】**  
  > In video presentation using rear projection technology, the "hotspot phenomenon," where the light source of the projector transmits through the screen and directly enters the eyes of the audience, is a major issue causing reduced visibility and uncomfortable glare. In this report, we focus on the straight line connecting the light source (projector) and the viewpoint (spectator), defined as the hotspot axis, and propose a geometric layout method to occlude this axis using physical obstacles such as a screen stand. We verify a structure that selectively blocks only the direct light path causing discomfort glare while securing the image light path by lowering the projector position and applying a steep elevation angle.
  > 
  > **Keywords**: Rear Projection, Hotspot Phenomenon, Geometric Occlusion, Glare, Projection Mapping
